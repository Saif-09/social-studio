# Formats (brand-neutral)

Pick by goal. Each brand's own proof (numbers, examples, topics) lives in its brand file and PERFORMANCE.md; check that first.

| Format | Gets | Structure | Build | Proven on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier list / "N signs"** | Saves | Hook question → 5 signs from mild to strong → payoff + CTA | drawn (mk_tier1.py) | astro brand 116k |
| **12→1 / top-N ranking of groups people belong to** (signs, cities, jobs, personality types) | Tags, shares | Hook "which X is the most Y?" → fast countdown, faces/badges → #1 → "tag your X" | drawn (mk_rank.py) | astro brand 106k / 5k shares |
| **Interactive test** ("do this now", "pause and pick") | Watch time, comments | Instruction at frame 1 → viewer acts → reveal per result | drawn/custom | astro brand 7.3k, 19 s watch |
| **Relatable pain** | Shares, saves | "Do you also…?" → why → one fix → CTA | drawn + text | astro brand 1.7–2.2k |
| **Story series with cliffhanger** | Follows, return viewers | Start mid-tension → story beats → stop at the peak → "Part N tomorrow, follow" | story template + ChatGPT art | astro brand (katha, best post of its day) |
| **Timely explainer** (festival, event, exam date, news) | Search + timely reach | What it is today → 3–5 steps/facts → what's next + CTA | story template | astro brand (Chhath days) |
| **Proof / testimonial** | Trust, conversions | Real chat/result screenshots (consent, names hidden) → offer → CTA | proof kit (edu brand) | needs real material |
| **Product/phone demo** | Shows the product | Problem → screen recording style flow → result | drawn app UI | under 2k for astro brand; use sparingly |
| **Quote / verse / fact card** | Saves, shares | One strong line, calm visual, voice, source | drawn text | faith brand (in progress) |

## Hook patterns (any brand)
- Suspense opener (default for explainers): tense moment on frame 1, "it's not X", a hidden cause hinted, "stay till #N" open loop; answer only after the hook. Never answer in line 1.
- Frame-1 tension mid-story ("She was taking the baby away 😳").
- Number + cost/sacrifice, paid off at the end ("36 hours, not one drop of water").
- Direct pain question ("Feel low for no reason?").
- An instruction ("Hold your wrist right now 👇").
- Contrarian line ("Everyone does X. It's wrong.").

## Comment drivers (any brand)
- One-tap reply that fits the audience (a blessing, "yes/no", their sign, a city).
- A "who/which" question pill mid-video.
- Pinned comment asking the same in one word.

## Pace (any brand)
≤45 s for stories, ≤25 s for lists; voice 1.1–1.25x; scene change every ~3 s; big captions in short chunks.
