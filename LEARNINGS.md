# Learnings (newest first; these override older guidance)

Format: date · [all] or [brand] · what · why · how to apply.

- 2026-11-17 · [all] · scripts/music_bed.py makes an original music bed (tension → drop → groove → end hit, downbeat anchored on "drop") with uv numpy/scipy; mix under the voice with ffmpeg sidechaincompress (music volume 0.42, ratio 12, threshold 0.015) so words stay clear. Check it with showspectrumpic: a fully red groove meant harsh broadband hats/claps (fixed with quieter hats + 10.5 kHz master low-pass).
- 2026-11-17 · [all] · A brand name ending in a stop consonant gets swallowed before a consonant (e.g. "Brandkit sent" heard as "Brandkai sent"), and a whole-file transcript hides it (context bias). Check brand names on a 1.5-2 s clip of each line; rephrase so a vowel follows ("a launch brand only sent…").
- 2026-11-17 · [all] · story_build TIMES has one more entry than scenes: [scene starts…, end-card start, end]. A page whose last scene ends at T[last+1] leaves the end-card slot (2-3 s) black; my contact sheet sampled before it and missed it. Make the last scene run to DURATION (`last = i === T.length - 3`) and always grab the final frame (`ffmpeg -sseof -1`) as a check.
- 2026-11-17 · [all] · Landscape and other sizes: put `<meta name="reel-size" content="1920x1080">` in the page; render.mjs and stills.mjs read it (default 1080x1920). reel.sh needs no change.
- 2026-11-15 · [all] · gemini-3.8-flash-tts reads a style direction aloud in English ("Read this as a sweet little child..." was spoken, 4/4 lines; length jumped 18 s to 32 s). Steered voices (accent, child) go on gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts, which obeys the direction silently. Check length first: a steered take much longer than the plain one means the direction was read. Child voice = 2.5 Pro + Leda/Zephyr (girl) or Puck (boy) + a one-line direction; pitch x1.18 (asetrate) for a younger sound.
- 2026-11-15 · [all] · Hooks need suspense, not the answer (user: "in reel starting we need to make suspense so that user waits"). Don't put the answer on the first frame or in line 1. Open on a tense moment (3:00 AM, dark room, a ticking clock, the baby's eyes snap open), name what it ISN'T ("not you, not always hunger"), hint at a hidden cause ("something isn't switched on yet"), and open a loop to the end ("stay till #4", a locked glowing card). Reveal the answer only in reason one. Answer teasers belong on the cover frame picked in the app, not in the playing reel. Template: another brand reel01.html HOOK 'C'.
- 2026-11-15 · [all] · Accent steering works on gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts: a one-line direction ("Speak in a warm, natural Indian English accent, like ...") + blank line + text gave an Indian English read, and Whisper showed the direction was NOT read aloud (4/4 voices). The user rejected all default (American-sounding) English voices for an Indian-run brand; offer accent-steered samples first.
- 2026-11-15 · [all] · Reels saved from Instagram with Hindi (Devanagari) filenames: macOS stores them NFD, so `ls | grep 'नवजात'` finds nothing and cp gets an empty name. Match with python unicodedata.normalize('NFC', name), or pick files by mtime.
- 2026-11-14 · [all] · A reel's end card was being cut by -shortest because the voice ended before the video · found while fixing Kharna · pad voice with apad (reel.sh now does); check final length = page DURATION / speed.
- 2026-11-14 · [all] · "chh"/whoosh at every scene change was never the lite model or an effect: Gemini replies with a WAV whose trailing C2PA chunk (~7.6 KB) we decoded as raw PCM = 0.17 s of -6 dB static at the end of every line · user heard it on every transition, twice · tts.mjs now parses the container; story_build trims only lines whose last 0.1 s is loud (old takes). The static also wrecked loudnorm (-16 LUFS) and likely the "blurry" voice.
- 2026-11-14 · [all] · No sound effects on story reels, not even soft ones · user: "so many sound effect which is not good" · voice only; reel.sh defaults to no sfx.
- 2026-11-14 · [all] · Pace: user found 66 s story "slower", "user will swipe" · Nani 1.22x, child 1.12x, gaps 0.16 s between scenes / 0.06 s within, end card 3.4 s · aim ≤45 s for a story part.
- 2026-11-14 · [all] · Transitions: hard punch-in cuts and slow crossfades looked bad · use the 0.35 s zoom-blur (templates/story-reel.html).
- 2026-11-13 · [all] · Need a hook that holds and drives comments · 2.5 s hook text at frame 1, mid-video who-question pill, end card "Comment mein likho …" + "Follow, Part N kal".
